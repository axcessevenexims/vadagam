Medicinal Properties & Health Benefits

Why Fermented Rice Fryums (Palaya Sadam Vathal) are South India's ultimate superfood side

1. Gut Health & Digestive Support

The natural fermentation process fosters beneficial probiotic bacteria that flush out toxins from the gut. Infused with cumin (jeera) and asafoetida (hing) to relieve indigestion, bloating, and gas issues.

Probiotic Gut Flora

2. Natural Energy & Vitamin B12

The fermentation process produces rich amounts of B-Complex vitamins and rare Vitamin B12. These provide instant natural energy to the body, strengthen the nervous system, and combat daily fatigue.

Vitamin B12 & Vitality

3. Ulcer Relief & Cooling

The natural cooling properties of fermented rice help regulate stomach acidity. Consumed with meals, mildly spiced fryums provide gentle soothing relief from stomach ulcers and acid reflux.

Acid Balance & Healing
